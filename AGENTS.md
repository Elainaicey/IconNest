# IconNest development guidelines

## Visual direction

- Keep the interface fresh, contemporary, light, and polished. Prefer calm surfaces, soft contrast, restrained gradients, and clear visual hierarchy over loud or overly saturated styling.
- The colors previously discussed—white, sakura pink, violet, sky blue, and khaki—are examples, not a fixed palette. Use any hue that suits the page, feature, state, or content.
- Use the full [Radix Themes color system](https://www.radix-ui.com/themes/docs/theme/color) as a reference when evolving the UI. Choose colors by semantic role and use their scales consistently:
  - steps 1–2 for page and subtle backgrounds;
  - steps 3–5 for interactive backgrounds;
  - steps 6–8 for borders, separators, and stronger interactive states;
  - steps 9–10 for solid controls and prominent accents;
  - steps 11–12 for accessible text.
- Pair accents with a suitable tinted neutral such as mauve, slate, sage, olive, or sand. Different features and icon sources may use complementary accents such as ruby, plum, iris, cyan, teal, jade, mint, amber, or orange when appropriate.
- Preserve accessible text contrast, visible focus states, coherent hover/active states, and a balanced overall palette. Do not add colors merely for decoration when they weaken hierarchy or usability.
- Treat the current palette as an evolving design system rather than a permanent constraint. Review color choices in the context of the complete page before finalizing UI changes.
